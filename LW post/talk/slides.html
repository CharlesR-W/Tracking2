What are the right variables?

Batterman, A Middle Way (2021); “Universality and RG Explanations” (2017) 2

0:30–1:55

  • Bottom-up neuron catalogs and top-down behavioral descriptions can both use the wrong variables.
  • Batterman’s middle-out wager: heterogeneous micro-details can realize autonomous mesoscale regularities; universality tells us which details are irrelevant.
  • For networks, the ontology should be earned by stability, prediction, and intervention—not assumed to be neurons, heads, or agents.

Neural networks keep doing weird things

Representative symptoms
Sun et al., “Transformer Layers as Painters” (2024) · Bansal, Nakkiran & Barak, model stitching (2021) 3

1:55–3:15

  • The painters result: lower and final layers are special, but middle layers can show striking uniformity; on some tasks they tolerate skipping, reordering, or parallel execution.
  • Stitching connectivity, mode connectivity, global steering directions, grokking, and redundant circuits are related symptoms: our default layer-by-layer story is not the right explanatory ontology.
  • These are not mechanisms by themselves; they are a class of phenomena a theory should retrodict.

Which dynamics survive the details?

Correct ontology = variables whose dynamics generalise across controlled changes. 4

3:15–4:25

  • The grand programme is not to collect analogies; it is to ask which dynamics are universal across seed, architecture, task, and scale.
  • A candidate object earns ontological status when it remains predictive under perturbation and improves intervention.
  • Today’s experiment is one narrow probe of the data-like object crossing each layer boundary.

Free body diagrams for neural networks

Methodological analogy: isolate a boundary, measure what crosses it, repeat. 6

5:40–6:35

  • The free-body move is organizational, not a claim that networks obey continuum mechanics.
  • Cut after a layer; everything before it becomes a data generator for everything after it.
  • Repeat the same measurement across depth to build a local picture.

Turn one layer into three datasets

Labels stay fixed; internal distributional structure is simplified. 8

  • Mean plus shared noise preserves class centres but removes class-specific covariance.

Same head at every cut

Vary PCA rank · capacity-match receivers · warm start vs reinitialize · independent seeds 12

12:10–13:20

  • The decisive statistical control inserts PCA-projected real activations before the Gaussian.
  • Full real to PCA real measures discarded directions; PCA real to Gaussian measures non-Gaussian structure in the same subspace; Gaussian to mean isolates class covariance.
  • The decisive architectural control attaches the same receiver at every cut, separating representation geometry from shrinking suffix capacity.

Then let the internal data move again

Two clocks at every cut: representation motion and suffix adaptation. 13

13:20–14:10

  • Ordinary end-to-end training makes the activation distribution move while the suffix learns.
  • Tracking means staying adapted to that moving distribution; resolving means learning more of the current distribution.
  • The frozen-prefix experiment measures only the resolving side. The longer-term target is a ratio of movement time to adaptation time across depth.
The larger wager

Build the ontology from what stays predictive

Today: one operational cut through the distributional dynamics of learning. 14

14:10–15:00

  • Return to the first diagram: the goal is a middle-out ontology of learned systems, based on stable dynamics rather than convenient parts lists.
  • This experiment treats the activation distribution at each interface as one candidate mesoscale object.
  • The immediate result is descriptive: low-order surrogates support much more similar learning late than early. The next controls determine whether that is a property of the data, the suffix, or both.
